Privacy

When you ask for my resume on this site, I keep what you type: your name, email, company, role and, if you add it, how you found me. I also keep whether you checked “Rod may contact me”, and when.

I use it to give you the resume link. If you checked the box, I may also reach out to you about it. I don't use it for anything else, and it's never sold or shared.

Everything is stored in my own database (Supabase). Your IP address is never stored; I keep only a salted hash of it to limit abuse.

If you checked the box, I keep your details for 12 months. If you didn't, I keep them for 90 days. Blocked or automated requests are kept for 90 days.

To see or delete what I hold about you, email admin@squared.pro. I'll answer within 30 days.

This site has no analytics, ads or third-party trackers, so it doesn't respond to Do Not Track signals; there's nothing to turn off.
