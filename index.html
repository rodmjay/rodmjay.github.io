About

I'm an architect and engineering leader with 21 years of taking systems from the first whiteboard sketch to production. I built the tooling and trained developers across the company to use it.

I'm good at getting different teams pulling in the same direction, setting standards that stick, and shaping how teams hire and test. I've worked in banking, real estate, marketing, logistics, procurement, government, healthcare and e-learning. I'm based in Salt Lake City and work in English and Spanish.
